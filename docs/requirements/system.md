# System requirements

Each row states one obligation and points at the specification that owns the detail; it never restates a schema, a list of values or a number. The columns are the same in every table:

- **Priority** is inherited from the business rows the item realises — the highest of them; an item that realises only a business rule is P0. Non-functional rows state their own.
- **Realises** names the `B-` and `RULE-` rows of [business requirements](/requirements/business.md) the item enforces; it is the golden source of traceability.
- **Flows** names the `FL-` flows of the [features](/features/index.md) in which the obligation is exercised; `all` marks an obligation every flow is subject to.
- **Entities** names the tables of the [SQL store](/architecture/sql-store.md) the obligation reads or writes; `none` marks one that touches no table.
- **Specified in** links the headings that own the detail.

Every row is verified by at least one `AC-` row of [acceptance criteria](/requirements/acceptance.md); the [traceability matrix](/requirements/traceability.md) is generated from these columns.
