# Features

* [Service configuration](service-configuration.md) - How an Admin defines a service, its signal questions and its versioned scoring settings - ICP criteria, weights, half-lives, disqualifiers and thresholds - tries a question before saving it, previews a scoring change and activates it without code, turns an open signal into a question, and maintains the Orange Systems facts drafts and interpretations may cite.
* [Accounts and discovery](accounts-and-discovery.md) - How Sales builds the target account list - CSV import with a dry run, manual entry, profile attributes, aliases, source addresses and minimal contacts - and how discovery suggests new accounts for a service, on request and every day, that a person accepts or rejects.
* [Signal pipeline](signal-pipeline.md) - How an account refresh turns public sources into findings, open signals, scores and their interpretation - fetch, normalise, triage, classify with the fast classifier, escalate the uncertain, quote the evidence, note other signals, score, interpret - on demand, in the daily cycle, after a question change and after a scoring change, with the Runs and Source plug-ins screens.
* [Prospect dashboard](prospect-dashboard.md) - How Sales works the ranked prospects of a service, opens an account's why view with the model's interpretation and every point of its score traced to criteria and quoted signals, records how far the team got with each company, drills into evidence and score history, how an Admin adds an exception to a disqualifier, and how alerts and the daily summary surface what changed.
* [Evaluation and feedback](evaluation-and-feedback.md) - How feedback on leads and signals corrects scores and becomes labelled data, how the team labels passages blind, and how an Admin runs a quality check that measures precision, recall, escalation and calibration against the release gate.
* [Outreach, engagement and CRM](outreach-and-crm.md) - How Sales drafts an email or LinkedIn InMail grounded in an account's quoted signals, the service's value proposition and Orange Systems' facts, edits and exports it without the product ever sending it, records whether the company answered - by hand or synced from HubSpot - and pushes an account's score and top signals to HubSpot.
* [Identity and access](identity-and-access.md) - How users sign in and out with local accounts, how sessions and lockout work, how the Sales and Admin roles are enforced, and how an Admin manages users.
* [Audit trail](audit-trail.md) - The append-only record of who changed what and of every classifier and LLM call with its model, prompt version, cost and outcome, as the Admin's Audit log screen filters and reads it back.
